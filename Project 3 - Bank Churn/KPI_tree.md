Bank Customer Churn
│
├── How many customers are leaving?
│   ├── Total customers
│   ├── Churned customers
│   ├── Retained customers
│   └── Churn rate = Churned customers ÷ Total customers
│
├── Which customer groups have higher churn?
│   ├── Churn rate by geography
│   ├── Churn rate by age group
│   ├── Churn rate by number of products
│   └── Churn rate by active-member status
│
├── How well do the prediction models identify churn?
│   ├── Accuracy
│   ├── Precision
│   ├── Recall
│   └── F1 score
│
└── Where could a retention offer be tested?
    ├── Number of high-risk customers
    ├── Estimated extra customers retained
    └── Estimated offer cost per extra customer retained
