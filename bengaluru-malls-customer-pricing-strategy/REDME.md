# Bengaluru Malls Customer Pricing Strategy

## Project Overview

The **Bengaluru Malls Customer Pricing Strategy** project focuses on analysing customer transactions, shopping behaviour, store performance, and pricing strategies across major shopping malls in Bengaluru.

The main objective is to understand how customer segments, store categories, discounts, purchase channels, and shopping days influence revenue and estimated gross profit.

This project uses data analysis and visualization techniques to transform transaction data into meaningful business insights and support better pricing and marketing decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour across different malls.
- Compare revenue and estimated gross profit across malls.
- Identify profitable store categories.
- Understand customer segments and their spending patterns.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on sales performance.
- Analyse weekday, weekend, and public holiday transactions.
- Support business decisions through interactive dashboards.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the supplied dataset:

- Mantri Square recorded the highest total revenue among the malls.
- Home & Lifestyle generated the highest estimated gross profit among store categories.
- The Family customer segment contributed the highest revenue.
- Discounting and customer purchasing patterns provide opportunities for further pricing analysis.
- Customer ratings can help identify areas where the shopping experience may need improvement.

## Business Recommendations

- Develop targeted promotions for different customer segments.
- Review discount strategies to improve profitability.
- Focus on high-performing store categories.
- Compare mall performance to identify business opportunities.
- Improve customer satisfaction using rating-based analysis.
- Monitor revenue and estimated gross profit regularly.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how customer transaction data can be converted into meaningful business information using data analytics and visualization.

The dashboard helps users understand mall performance, customer behaviour, pricing patterns, and profitability. It also supports data-driven decision-making for pricing and marketing strategies.

## Limitations

- The dataset contains synthetic educational records.
- Results are based only on the available 250 transactions.
- Estimated gross profit is not audited accounting profit.
- Findings should not be treated as actual market performance.
