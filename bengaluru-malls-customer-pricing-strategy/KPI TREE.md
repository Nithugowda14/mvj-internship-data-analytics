# KPI Tree – Bengaluru Malls Customer Pricing Strategy

## 1. Main Business Objective

**To improve mall revenue and profitability through customer analysis, pricing analysis, discount evaluation, and store performance analysis.**

## 2. KPI Tree Structure

```text
                 MAIN BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
          Improve Revenue and Profitability
                           |
        +------------------+------------------+
        |                  |                  |
   Revenue Growth     Profitability      Customer & Pricing
        |                  |                  |
   - Total Revenue    - Gross Profit     - Customer Segment
   - Transaction      - Gross Profit     - Customer Rating
     Count              Margin           - Average Discount %
   - Average Revenue  - Profit by        - Selling Price
     per Transaction    Store Category   - Purchase Channel
   - Revenue by Mall  - Estimated Cost   - Discount Impact
   - Revenue by
     Customer Segment
```

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### A. Revenue KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Transaction Count
- Average Revenue per Transaction
- Revenue by Mall
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Revenue by Store Category

### B. Profitability KPIs
- Estimated Gross Profit
- Estimated Gross Profit Margin
- Estimated Profit by Store Category
- Estimated Profit by Mall
- Estimated Unit Cost

### C. Customer KPIs
- Customer Segment
- Customer Rating
- Purchase Channel
- Customer Purchase Behaviour

### D. Pricing KPIs
- Average Discount Percentage
- List Price
- Selling Price
- Discount Impact on Revenue and Estimated Profit

## 4. KPI Formulas

| KPI | Formula |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | SUM(Revenue) |
| Estimated Gross Profit | SUM(Estimated Gross Profit) |
| Gross Profit Margin | (Estimated Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100 |
| Average Discount % | AVERAGE(Discount %) |
| Average Revenue per Transaction | Total Revenue / Transaction Count |

## 5. Business Purpose

The KPI Tree helps identify:
- Which mall generates the highest revenue.
- Which store category contributes the highest estimated profit.
- Which customer segment generates more revenue.
- How discounts affect selling prices and estimated profitability.
- How customer ratings and purchase channels support business analysis.

## 6. Expected Business Outcome

The analysis supports better pricing decisions, discount planning, customer targeting, and mall performance evaluation.

**Note:** The dataset contains simulated educational records. Gross profit values are estimates and should not be treated as actual company financial results.
