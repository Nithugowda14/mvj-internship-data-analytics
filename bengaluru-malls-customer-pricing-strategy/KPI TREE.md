Bro, for your **Bengaluru Malls Customer Pricing Strategy** project, a KPI Tree shows how the main business goal is connected to smaller measurable KPIs.

## **KPI Tree – Mall Pricing Strategy**

**MAIN BUSINESS GOAL**

Increase Mall Revenue and Profitability

**Revenue Growth**

Sales performance

**Profitability**

Profit performance

**Revenue KPIs**

- Total Revenue
- Transaction Count
- Average Revenue per Transaction
- Revenue by Mall
- Revenue by Customer Segment

**Profit KPIs**

- Estimated Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Estimated Unit Cost
- Profit by Store Category

**Customer KPIs**

- Customer Segment
- Customer Rating
- Purchase Channel

**Pricing KPIs**

- Average Discount %
- List Price
- Selling Price
- Discount Impact

## **Important KPI formulas**

| KPI                             | Formula                          |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue                   | Sum of Revenue                   |
| Estimated Gross Profit          | Sum of Gross Profit              |
| Gross Profit Margin             | (Gross Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100   |
| Average Discount                | Sum of Discount % ÷ Transactions |
| Average Revenue per Transaction | Revenue ÷ Transactions           |
